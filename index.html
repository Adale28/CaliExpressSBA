Tips for Reducing Travel Costs

Maximize your savings when flying across the islands with our top budget-friendly travel practices:

  • Book Directly Online: Save on third-party broker fees by utilizing our web portal to secure lower web-only promo fares.
  • Travel Mid-Week: Mid-week departures (Tuesdays and Wednesdays) typically feature significantly lower seat pricing than weekend flights.
  • Pack Efficiently: Avoid checking multiple bags. Utilize a single, standard carry-on bag within weight thresholds to bypass excess baggage fees.
  • Join Frequent Flyer Programs: Accumulate travel miles on every segment to unlock free seat upgrades, priority check-in, and complimentary bag allowances.
